# Walmart Sales Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze Walmart's sales performance across regions, products, and customers — turning raw transactional data into a single, drill-down-ready view for business decision-making.

## Overview

This dashboard consolidates key retail metrics into one dark-themed, single-page report, letting stakeholders explore sales trends, top and bottom performers, and customer behavior without touching the underlying data.

Key headline metrics:
- Total Customers: 51,290
- Total Sales: $126.9M

## Features

- Total Sales at Different Places — a trend line tracking daily/periodic sales fluctuations across locations, highlighting spikes and dips over time.
- Monthly Customer Growth — combo chart (bar + line) showing count of customers alongside month-over-month customer growth rate (Jan–Dec).
- Total Sales in Every Month — a labeled column chart with exact sales values called out for each month, making YoY/MoM comparison easy at a glance.
- Top Performing Products — horizontal bar chart ranking the best-selling products by sales value.
- Low Performing Products — horizontal bar chart isolating underperforming SKUs to flag for review or discontinuation.
- Profit vs. Sales — a comparative line/area view of profit against total sales to surface margin trends.
- Avg Ticket Size – Top 10 Customers — a pie chart breaking down average order value contribution by month/customer segment for the top 10 customers.
- Country & Sales lookup panel — a scrollable reference table of countries and corresponding sales figures for granular lookups.

## Interactivity

- Slicers/Filters for Country, Date, and Customer, enabling dynamic drill-down across every visual on the report simultaneously.
- All charts are cross-filtered — selecting a value in one slicer updates every visual on the dashboard.

## Tools & Techniques Used

- Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, PivotCharts)
- Slicers for multi-dimensional filtering (Country / Date / Customer)
- Data cleaning and formatting for a large multi-thousand-row retail dataset
- Custom dashboard theming (dark background, high-contrast green accents) for a clean executive-facing look

## Key Insights Enabled

- Identify seasonal sales spikes and underperforming periods at a glance
- Pinpoint top vs. low performing products to guide inventory and marketing decisions
- Track customer growth trends month over month
- Compare profit against sales to monitor margin health
- Drill into any country, date range, or customer for a focused view
